## Created by Matthew Martin

I’m Matthew Martin, creator of **[Mawkingbird](https://mawkingbird.com/)**, a Mastodon client for new users and power users. I built this guide to help you discover the web clients available and choose one you’ll enjoy using.

Mawkingbird brings a familiar, Twitter-inspired experience to Mastodon, with Bluesky and RSS integration, starter kits, and plenty to explore before you create an account. This guide is also a place to introduce that work.

## Find the right fit

Different people want different things from a client. Some want a simple place to read; others want several accounts, columns, or more ways to compose. These guides explain the strengths and trade-offs and link you to the software so you can try it.

Start with [your needs](/), browse [all clients](/clients/), or [meet Mawkingbird](/clients/mawkingbird/).

## How comparisons work

Feature records support the comparisons. Ratings summarize feature coverage and project signals; they are useful within a shortlist, while your priorities determine the fit. [The methodology](/methodology/) explains the details.

The guide covers browser-based Mastodon-compatible clients. Facts and corrections are maintained in the [public repository](https://github.com/matthewdeanmartin/mastodon_clients).
